Stand-alone programming exercises on Rosalind

Open education is important to us. If you have the print book, or you are reading the free chapters on this site, you can solve the book's programming exercises for free on Rosalind, named in honor of Rosalind Franklin.

Using the book in your own course

Both authors, and many instructors who have adopted the book, use the interactive text and lecture videos for a flipped classroom: students work through the text on Cogniterra, which frees class time for exercises that reinforce it. We describe this approach in a PLOS Computational Biology article.

We offer discounted pricing to license the interactive textbook for university courses, and we are happy to add you to our network of instructors, which shares PowerPoint slides for every chapter and other materials. Contact us to learn more.

Algorithms beyond biology

If you want to learn more about algorithms in general, we think you will love the edX MicroMasters Program in Algorithms and Data Structures, which integrates some material from this book while teaching many fundamental algorithms across computer science.
